OKN Viewer ("the App") respects your privacy.

#### 1. Collection of Information and On-device Storage

The App does not require account registration. The Developer does not collect users’ personal information through the App or automatically transmit it to the Developer’s external servers.

With your permission, the recording feature uses the front camera to record eye movements. Videos are saved in the App’s on-device storage, where you can play or delete them from the recording history. Display preferences are also stored on your device.

If you choose to save a video to your photo library, the App saves it there with your permission. That copy is handled according to your Apple Photos, backup and related settings. Deleting a video inside the App does not delete a copy saved to your photo library.

#### 2. Third-Party Services and External Transmission

The App does not use Google AdMob, Firebase, advertising services or user-behavior analytics. Recorded videos are not automatically sent to external servers.

If you send information through email or other services, the policies of the recipient and those services apply. This policy describes the App’s data handling; it does not mean that your device or Apple services perform no data processing.

#### 3. Contact

Email addresses and information voluntarily provided through support inquiries are used only as necessary to respond. You may also contact this address with questions or requests concerning the handling or deletion of information.

This Privacy Policy may be updated as necessary. Changes take effect when the updated policy is published on this page.
