#### Android version (updated October 14, 2026)

CarSplit for Android is intended for adult organizers, parents and drivers aged 18 and over. Children may be included in a roster; enter only necessary information and take care when choosing recipients.

Rosters, rules, results, history and settings are stored on the device, without synchronization to a developer-operated cloud server. Uninstalling removes local app data. CSV/JSON backups, shared images and messages are managed by the storage locations or recipients the user chooses.

Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) is used to serve ads. Approximate location derived from IP addresses, advertising and other identifiers, interactions, diagnostics and performance information may be sent to Google for advertising, measurement and fraud prevention. Advertising identifiers can be managed in Android advertising privacy settings. Google consent management (UMP) is used where applicable; ad privacy choices can be changed in the app when required.

Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics collection are disabled by default and can be enabled or disabled separately in Settings. When enabled, corresponding Google services may receive screen/action/count events, device and installation identifiers, approximate region, and crash/diagnostic information. Analytics may also include automatically collected purchase/subscription events, such as product IDs and prices. Developer-defined Analytics events do not include roster names, individual grade information, free-text messages or individual member IDs. Related SDK processing and retention follow Google's policies.

Google Play processes purchases and subscriptions; the developer does not receive card information. The app checks purchases to provide premium features, and stores support purchase counts and purchase-token hashes locally to prevent duplicate processing. Consumed support counts are not restored after reinstallation. iOS purchase rights do not automatically transfer.

Sample review mode uses separate in-memory data and grants no actual purchase entitlement. Changes to the samples are discarded when the mode ends.
